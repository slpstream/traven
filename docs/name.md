# Traven naming, and why

As a name for an editor, "Traven" represents the philosophy of what it does and where it can fit.

It takes its name from [B. Traven](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/32530.B_Traven), the author of *The Treasure of the Sierra Madre*, one of the most successfully anonymous writers of the twentieth century, but also a man whose true identity was never conclusively established despite decades of investigation.

B. Traven was an anonymous author who wrote about ordinary people fighting systems that would grind them down, whose manuscripts arrived at publishers without a return address, who communicated through intermediaries, and who built a body of work that outlasted every attempt to attach a name to it. He spent his entire career proving that the work outlasts the author, that anonymity is not absence, that you can build something real and meaningful and then walk away from it completely and it still exists on its own terms. 

For a privacy-first, local/selfhosted WYSIWYM editor that is made to be embedded whereever full-featured Markdown editing is required, that's an apt namesake. It is for developers who prefer to selfhost because they understand what ownership actually means in practice.

With that, we hand Traven to the commons.  MIT licensed, use it freely.
